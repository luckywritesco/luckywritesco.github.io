About

I am Lucky David, a writer who believes the hardest part of a story is rarely the story itself. It is the silence around it.

I have been writing since childhood, when a paper diary held every tear, every joy and every secret. I know what it costs to keep a story quiet, and what it feels like to finally say it out loud.

I also hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science, so I am comfortable with the technical side of publishing, from formatting to web pages.

Now I write brand origin stories, script kits, manifestos and memoirs for executives, founders, personal brands and first-time authors who are ready to say theirs. I find the small, true details that hold the whole story, and I keep the honesty in. Read how it started.

The Secret in My School Socks

At a Nigerian primary school, a young girl passes the sock inspection at the gate, then quietly puts those same socks to a different use. She hides her first period behind a navy uniform and a locked mouth, until a white skirt and a plate of her father’s leftover food give her away. A story about shame, silence, and the things we learn to hide before we have words for them.
