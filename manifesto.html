We Are Pathfinders

A manifesto sample by Lucky David

They tell us to follow the path most people have trodden. It is the easier route, because everything is familiar. Why start a new one? We might not win.

But that familiar path was first taken by someone no one believed in. It exists because a person once refused to wait for permission, and it made it possible for others to follow.

So we believe:

Every pathfinder threatens the status quo. The old way is no longer enough.

Starting without a safety net is still starting. When no one has done it before, and no success is guaranteed, someone has to go first.

Breaking new ground is for the future of others. We are not only building for ourselves.

Wrong turns are part of the map. When there are no maps, we will have faith in the unknown.

We will not follow because it is safe. We will not stop because it is hard. We will set our own trail, and we will find a way forward.
