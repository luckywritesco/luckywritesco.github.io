My 7-Day Writing Challenge

Seven days, seven pieces: six poems and a story, written daily to build range, voice and discipline.

Day 1

Blue Hour

A colloquial term for a colourful spectacleThe phase of twilight when the sun sits just below the horizonGod’s very design, the dynamics of nature

I gaze at the wonder God has createdMy morning slowly comes alive beneath a sky of deep, cool blueThe calmness that fills the surroundings and the scent of the morning dew

The bridge between the warm golden hour and complete darkness,Appearing before the sun rises and after it setsIt tells me it’s time to start my day and leads me right home when the day is done

For about 20 to 30 minutes, the sky becomes an expression of creativity,The love that fills the heavens showing the glory of the Mighty OneThe blue hour says it’s time to reflect and refresh

Natural lighting at dawn and the last stages of twilight at duskPlay around, let your imagination come to lifeCreate memories, and don’t let your memory card be your limit

Day 2

Equinox

Like poles repel,Unlike poles attract.Now I understand that opposites truly attract.We are from different backgrounds and cultures,Two unlike poles with strong magnetic fields,Pulling each other closer.

I’m in love.

I am warm and you are cold,Like night to day.So what if you are different?My heart keeps leaning toward you.No other man but you.

The equinox, a point of transition only love can balance.Even though we are different, we are equal before our maker.Even at the equinox, night and day are nearly equal in length,Evidence that God acknowledges our equality.

I consider us equal.Let me love you and be with you.In your arms is where my heart wants to be,and from there we set our journey.

Day 3

Freedom

Freedom, the most sought-after pursuit of life,One of humanity’s oldest longings.It comes in the form of free will and choice.Everyone wants it; everyone needs it.

Freedom from oppressors, freedom from self.Freedom from oppressors, because you have a right to life.Freedom from self: how else, if not through restraint?What if the walls that kept us dependent are protecting us?

The sound of freedom stirs most hearts,Their minds echo, “I want to be free.”I look around and wonder: do we deserve the freedom we seek?What have we done with what has been entrusted to us?The more we gain, the more our arrogance grows.

What will you do when nothing is holding you back?

Day 4

Home

I love sunsets, because I’ll be going home to my lover.My smile widens as I walk out the door of the office.I am the clock-out fairy, because my hands can’t wait to hold you.The street I once dreaded leaving home has become my happy road to you.

Where else would I be if not with you?You are my home, for my home is not a place.My home is where you are, right there in your heart.Through the stormy rain, the hot sun, and the dusty harmattan,All roads lead to you.

With you, I belong, I feel safe, and I get to be myself.The memories we have shared, and the ones we will create, keep me running to you.There is no place I would rather be than with you.My home is not a place. My home is you.

Day 5

Creative Block

I lie and stare at the ceiling.Yes, I want to write, and I do think and imagine.But why can’t I write?Doubts creep in.My self-esteem is slipping.I feel short of words.

When the block came, I questioned my thoughts.I feared judgment, criticism, and mistakes.My head felt full and weary from doing nothing.Everything distracted me, yet nothing came.I didn’t know if I could handle this.There was no energy in me.

Then I found a hidden jewelin my messy work, in walking, in the creative nature of God, and in change.It taught me patience and peace.I accepted the calm.I took a deep breath,and my mind said, “Write me.”

I started writing my thoughts down,and the blocks began crumbling.Before I knew it, I had written a whole page.It’s a fresh start.

Day 6

Pathfinder

“Follow the path that most people have trodden.It is the easier route, because everything is familiar.Why start a new route? You might not win.Look at those who have tried and failed.You think you will succeed?”

The familiar path, you say?That path was first taken by a person no one believed in.That path made it possible for others to follow.And that path, you say, I should follow?Now I want to set my own trail.

Every pathfinder threatens the status quo.The old way is no longer enough.Even when there’s no safety net,when no one has done it before,when no success is guaranteed,I need to break new ground for the future of others.When there are no maps and wrong turns become inevitable,even then, I will have faith in the unknown.

Because it’s a new way forward,I will find a way to survive.
